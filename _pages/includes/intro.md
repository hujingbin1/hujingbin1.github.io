## 👋 About Me

Hi, I’m **Jingbin Hu (胡景斌)**, a graduate student and researcher with the Audio, Speech and Language Processing Group ([ASLP@NPU](https://www.nwpu-aslp.org/)) at [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://www.nwpu.edu.cn/), Xi’an, China, advised by Prof. [Lei Xie](http://www.nwpu-aslp.org/lxie/).

My research interests include **audio generation, representation learning, and post-training**. I work on expressive and controllable speech generation, speech and multimodal large language models, and audio representations. I enjoy building open research systems alongside developing new methods.

You can find my complete and up-to-date publication profile on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=kA8XJkYAAAAJ&hl=en) and my code on [GitHub](https://github.com/hujingbin1).
