## 💻 Selected Research Projects

- **[VoiceSculptor](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/VoiceSculptor)** — Instruction-based voice design and high-fidelity voice cloning, developed with ASLP Lab and collaborators.
- **[WenetSpeech-Wu](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/WenetSpeech-Wu-Repo)** — A large-scale Wu dialect speech corpus with multidimensional annotations, benchmarks, and models.
- **[M5-TTS](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/M5-TTS)** — A compact multilingual and multi-dialect TTS language model designed for efficient inference and voice cloning.
- **[DRAMA-Eval](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/DRAMA-Eval)** — A bilingual benchmark for context-aware, multi-turn, multi-speaker speech generation.
- **[OmniCodec](https://github.com/ASLP-lab/OmniCodec)** — A low-frame-rate universal audio codec with semantic–acoustic disentanglement.
